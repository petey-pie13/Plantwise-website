About the Project

Empowering Smallholder Farmers

PlantWise bridges the gap between scientific agricultural knowledge and the farmer on the ground — providing localized, accessible recommendations for crop selection and disease management across Kenya's 47 counties.

Developed as part of a Computer Science research initiative by Peter O. Audi, the system targets the smallholder farming majority to help curb food insecurity in Kenya's arid and semi-arid regions.

Core Modules

What's Being Built

Two intelligent subsystems forming the backbone of PlantWise's decision-support capabilities.

Ecological Crop Advisory

A fuzzy-logic algorithm that maps each farmer's county-level ecological data — soil pH, annual rainfall, altitude, and temperature — to the optimal staple crop from a curated database of 11 varieties. The system gracefully handles data imprecision, reflecting real-world agricultural variability.

Fuzzy Logic 11 Staple Crops County Mapping Soil pH Rainfall Data
Hybrid AI Disease Diagnosis

A two-layer diagnostic engine combining the Kindwise Plant.id API (cloud-based visual AI) with a locally hosted MySQL Knowledge Base of 50+ diseases and their culturally appropriate treatments. Offline fallback ensures reliability in areas with limited connectivity.
